# Data gap completion checklist

## Goal and completion predicate

Fill each public dashboard gap that has a free source with documented public redistribution permission. A public snapshot must contain current values with observation dates and attribution, or a specific missing or degraded reason. No personal-use source, old unauthorized history, or AI brief may remain. The candidate must pass source-rights, schema, and dashboard checks; production Pages must match the candidate before issue #287 is complete.

## Scope and rigor

The change spans 28 macro series, five configured US equities, eleven A-shares, two sectors, twenty themes, energy, commodities, four calendar events, twenty news items, and risk model 1.2.0. Rigor is high because the result republishes third-party data to a public Pages site. The known blocker is the lack of a verified public redistribution grant for equities, sectors, A-shares, and four credit/volatility series.
